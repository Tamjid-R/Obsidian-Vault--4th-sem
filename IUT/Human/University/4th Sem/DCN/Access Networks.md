# Access Networks

An **Access Network** is the physical link that connects an end system (host) to the first router (also known as the edge router) on the path from the end system to any other distant end system.

## Types of Access Networks

### 1. Residential Access
These connect homes to the Internet Service Provider (ISP).

- **DSL (Digital Subscriber Line):**
    - **Technology:** Uses existing copper telephone lines.
    - **Nature:** Dedicated line to the Central Office (CO).
    - **Multiplexing:** Uses FDM to separate data and voice.
- **Cable Internet:**
    - **Technology:** Uses **HFC (Hybrid Fiber Coax)** — fiber to the neighborhood, then coax to the home.
    - **Nature:** Shared medium among neighbors.
- **FTTH (Fiber to the Home):**
    - **Technology:** Optical fiber directly to the residence.
    - **Nature:** High speed, usually dedicated or split via passive optical networks (PON).

### 2. Enterprise & Home Access
- **Ethernet:** Usually 100Mbps, 1Gbps, or 10Gbps via twisted-pair copper. Used in universities and companies.
- **Wireless (Wi-Fi):** Based on IEEE 802.11. Connects wireless end systems to a base station (access point).

### 3. Mobile Access
- **4G/5G:** Connects mobile devices via a wide-area radio access network provided by cellular carriers.

### Feynman Explanation
Think of an **Access Network** as the **"Driveway"** of your house.
- The **Internet Core** is like the massive global highway system.
- To get your car (data) onto that highway, you first need a path from your garage to the main road. 
- Some driveways are private and made of old gravel (**DSL**), some are shared with neighbors like a communal parking lot (**Cable**), and some are high-speed private tunnels (**FTTH**). 
- No matter what it's made of, the "Access Network" is just that specific path that gets you from your front door to the start of the public road system.

### Examples
- **Home Wi-Fi:** Connecting your phone to your router.
- **Campus Ethernet:** Plugging your laptop into the wall jack in a university library.
- **Mobile Data:** Your phone connecting to a cell tower while you're on a bus.

---

Q. What is an access network?
ANS: An access network is the series of wires or wireless signals that connect an end system (like a smartphone, laptop, or server) to the edge router of its Internet Service Provider (ISP). It represents the "last mile" of communication infrastructure between the user and the global internet core.
