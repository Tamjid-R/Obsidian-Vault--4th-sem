# IMAP: Internet Mail Access Protocol

IMAP is the modern way we read email. It is a **Mail Access Protocol**.

---

## 1. IMAP vs. POP3 (The Old Way)
*   **POP3 (Download and Delete):** Like taking a letter out of a physical mailbox and bringing it home. Once it's on your computer, it's gone from the server. If you check your mail on another device, it's empty!
*   **IMAP (Remote Storage):** Like looking at your letters through a window at the post office. The mail stays on the server. You can organize it into folders, and those folders look the same on your phone, tablet, and PC.

---

## 2. Key Features
*   **User State:** IMAP remembers which emails you've read, which ones you've flagged, and what folders you've created.
*   **Efficiency:** You can download just the "Subject" line of 100 emails without downloading the actual content or attachments until you click on one.

---

## 3. Technical Specs
*   **Transport Protocol:** TCP
*   **Port Number:** 143 (IMAPS/SSL uses 993)
*   **Function:** Retrieval from server to User Agent (UA).
